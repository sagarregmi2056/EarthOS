
I am building EarthOS, an open-source, planet-scale operating system to make Earth fully observable, optimizable, and collaborative. The vision is to create a "HUD for humanity" to monitor and manage Earth's resources, climate, and ecosystems in real-time. Below is a detailed overview of the project, its modules, tech stack, and target audience. I need your assistance to accelerate development, optimize code, design architecture, and generate actionable insights for specific tasks.

Project Overview Vision: Transform Earth into a data-driven, collaborative system for sustainability, providing real-time insights into pollution, agriculture, wildlife, energy, climate, and citizen-contributed data. Core Modules:





PolluTrack: Real-time air/water pollution tracking using IoT sensors and satellite data.



AgriAI: Crop health monitoring, rainfall prediction, and pest warnings for farmers.



EcoWatch: Wildlife tracking via satellites, drones, and ground sensors.



EnergyGrid: Visualizes global/regional energy consumption and production.



ClimateLens: Tracks climate change impacts and regional carbon footprints.



CitizenNode: Crowdsourced data from smartphones and IoT sensors (like Waze for Earth).



AI Advisor: Recommends actions for emissions reduction, urban planning, and resource conservation.

Tech Stack:





Frontend: React, CesiumJS (for 3D globe visualization)



Backend: Node.js, Python (for AI), PostgreSQL, TimeScaleDB (for time-series data)



Data Sources: NASA/ESA APIs, Planet Labs, Google Earth Engine



Sensors: LoRaWAN, NB-IoT for citizen-contributed data



AI: TensorFlow/PyTorch for predictive and optimization models



Infrastructure: Open-source on GitHub, decentralized access via IPFS



Governance: DAO-style community governance for feature prioritization

Target Audience:





NGOs/Governments: Policy insights



Scientists: Open datasets



Startups: Platform for eco-solutions



Citizens: Climate awareness and actionable micro-tips
